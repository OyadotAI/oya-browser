# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report it privately through a GitHub security advisory:
https://github.com/OyadotAI/oya-browser/security/advisories/new

Please do not open a public issue. Include what you found, how to reproduce it,
and what an attacker could do with it.

## Compliance

How the service is secured, its HIPAA status and its subprocessors are on the
trust center: https://oyabrowser.com/trust. For a business associate agreement
or a security questionnaire, write to sales@getoya.ai.
